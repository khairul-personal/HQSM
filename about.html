Who we are

Knowledge and compassion, side by side.

Holy Quran School & Madrasah is a beacon of education and spirituality, providing a nurturing environment for students to excel academically and deepen their understanding of Islamic values.

With a dedicated faculty and a comprehensive curriculum, we strive to cultivate well-rounded individuals who embrace knowledge and compassion. We are committed to empowering our students to become lifelong learners and compassionate leaders in their communities.

The school operates on its own funding and student tuition fees. Every rule in our fee and attendance policy exists for one reason: financial stability to keep raising the standard of education.

Our mission

Break the divide. Build the nation.

“The mission of this institution is to break the traditional three-stream education system — Qawmi, Alia and general education — and, in the face of modern global challenges, to take practical steps toward creating a group of thoughtful, morally upright and skilled citizens who follow the Quran and Sunnah for national development and progress.”

From the school’s mission statement

Qawmi

Depth of faith

The Quran, Hadith and classical Islamic learning.

Alia

Structured study

Arabic and Islamic subjects in an organised curriculum.

General

Modern skills

The subjects today’s world asks of every student.

Thoughtful, upright, skilled citizensFollowing the Quran and Sunnah, serving the nation
The principal

Md Saifullah

Principal Md Saifullah leads Holy Quran School & Madrasah with wisdom, compassion and dedication. Under his guidance, students excel academically while embracing Islamic values.

With a comprehensive curriculum and a nurturing environment, he fosters the holistic growth of every student. His leadership inspires unity, respect and a commitment to excellence across the school.

Speaking with the principal. Guardians raise important matters directly with the principal. Contact the office to arrange a meeting — guardians do not enter classrooms during teaching hours.

What we teach every day

Values drawn from our student regulations.

بسم الله

Begin with Allah’s name

Every task starts in the name of Allah, and daily prayer is part of school life.

الصدق

Truthfulness

Students always tell the truth — even when they have done something seriously wrong.

الأدب

Respect

Greet and respect parents, teachers and elders; be affectionate to those younger.

الاجتهاد

Diligence

Work hard with self-confidence, and trust in Allah for success.

الطهارة

Cleanliness

Keep body, clothes and classroom clean and pure.

الصحبة

Good company

Avoid bad company, reject injustice and keep good relations with everyone.
