Students & parents

Guardians are a child’s first and best teachers. These guidelines explain how students, families and the school work together.

Student regulations

How our students conduct themselves

Character

  • Begin all tasks in the name of Allah.
  • Respect and greet parents, teachers and elders.
  • Be affectionate towards younger people.
  • Always tell the truth, even about a serious mistake.
  • Avoid bad company and reject unjust behaviour.
  • Work hard with self-confidence, and trust in Allah for success.
  • Maintain good relations with everyone.
  • Pray daily.

In class

  • Arrive five minutes before class starts.
  • Wear the designated uniform.
  • Keep body and clothes clean and pure.
  • Join assembly with discipline, including Quran recitation and physical training.
  • Stay seated and quiet when classes change.
  • Prepare daily lessons and bring notebooks, pens and books.
  • Keep your own classroom clean.

Exams

  • Take every monthly test and both semester exams.
  • Do not seek help from others during exams.
  • Return monthly test answer sheets signed by a guardian within two days.
Exam calendar

Getting help

  • Take any problem to your class or subject teacher.
  • Inform the principal about important matters.
  • The institution’s decision on any matter is final.

Guardian guidelines

For respected guardians

Guardians are expected to help the school with their child’s education. These guidelines help secure every child’s bright future.

Every school day

  • Send your child by 8:30 AM in a clean uniform with all supplies.
  • Make sure they attend on time and take part in physical training.
  • Check books, notebooks, chalk, duster, pens and pencils before they leave, and replace supplies before they run out.
  • Check handwriting and homework daily; if incomplete, finish it at home or tell the class teacher.
  • Look after hygiene: nails, teeth, hair, face and eyes.
  • Bring your child to and from the madrasah — the school is not responsible for accidents on the way.

Communication

  • Meet the class teacher at least once a month about progress.
  • Write concerns in the diary and inform the school through the class teacher.
  • If your child is ill, inform the principal.
  • For any problem, inform the principal directly.
  • Avoid leave; urgent leave needs a written application to the principal.

On campus

  • Do not enter classes during teaching hours — contact the office to arrange a meeting.
  • Female guardians wear a burqa and observe Shari’ah-compliant hijab when visiting.
  • Keep a good, respectful relationship with all teachers and staff.

At home

  • Always pray for your child and never curse them.
  • Pray regularly and teach your children to do the same.
  • After prayers, recite Masnoon supplications and Surahs from Fazail with your children.

Exams, payments and discipline

  • Every exam matters — results are based on an average.
  • Never make a payment without a receipt.
  • Keep the fee and result cards safe.
  • The school may expel a student for an unpardonable offence to maintain discipline.
