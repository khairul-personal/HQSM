Welcome

A beacon of education and spirituality.

Holy Quran School & Madrasah provides a nurturing environment where students excel academically and deepen their understanding of Islamic values. With dedicated teachers and a comprehensive curriculum, we cultivate well-rounded young people who embrace both knowledge and compassion.

Principal Md Saifullah leads the school with wisdom, compassion and dedication. Under his guidance, students grow as lifelong learners and as compassionate leaders in their communities.

Our mission

Three streams of education. One school.

Bangladesh has long divided education into Qawmi, Alia and general streams. We exist to break that divide — raising thoughtful, upright and skilled citizens who follow the Quran and Sunnah and serve the nation.

Qawmi tradition

Depth of faith

The Quran, Hadith and classical Islamic learning at the heart of every day.

Alia tradition

Structured study

Arabic and Islamic subjects taught through an organised madrasah curriculum.

General education

Modern skills

The academic subjects every student needs to thrive in today’s world.
