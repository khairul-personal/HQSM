Integrated curriculum

From mathematics to Islamic studies

Our students are prepared for both worlds. The curriculum spans general academic subjects and Islamic learning, so no child has to choose between faith and future.

Islamic learning

  • The Quran, with recitation part of every morning assembly
  • Islamic studies rooted in the Quran and Sunnah
  • Daily prayer and Islamic manners (adab)

General academics

  • A comprehensive modern curriculum
  • Subjects including mathematics
  • Physical training during assembly

Full subject list by class. The office can share the classes offered and subjects for each level — call 01911-020507.

How scores are calculated

Monthly work and main exams both count

Two monthly tests are averaged, then added to the main exam score for each subject.

Exam rules

What every student must do

Taking part

  • All students take every monthly test, the first semester and the second semester exams.
  • Do not seek help from others during exams.

After each test

  • Return monthly test answer sheets signed by a guardian within two days.
  • Keep fee and result cards safe.

Fees and exams. If fees are outstanding for two consecutive months, the student cannot sit the semester or annual exams until all dues are cleared. Payment rules
