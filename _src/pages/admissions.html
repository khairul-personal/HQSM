Fees & payments

Monthly dues, paid in advance.

The school runs on its own funding and student tuition. All dues for the current month, including tuition and coaching fees, are paid in advance by the 7th.

When you payLate fee
By the 7th of the monthNone
By the 15th৳50
By the 30th৳100
Together with next month’s fees৳200
Two consecutive months unpaid — added to the third month’s fees৳400

Never make a payment without a receipt.

{{info}}

Two months unpaid: the student cannot sit the first semester, second semester or annual exams until all dues are cleared.

{{info}}

Three months unpaid: the student’s name is removed from the attendance register. To continue, re-enrol by paying all dues plus a fine equal to one month’s tuition.

Attendance

  • Absence without written permission or a medical certificate is fined ৳10 per day.
  • 15 consecutive days’ absence without permission cancels the admission.
  • For urgent leave, apply in writing to the principal.
